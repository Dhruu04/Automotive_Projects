Real Driving NOx Emissions vs Exhaust Temperature
Shows how exhaust temperature below 180°C creates temporary cold-start emissions
RDE Emissions
What This Means for the Company:

When the exhaust catalyst operates above 220°C, catalytic efficiency exceeds 95%, keeping tailpipe NOx well below the 60 mg/km Euro 7 limit. Cold starts in stop-and-go city traffic cause brief emissions spikes.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Install 48V electric exhaust heaters that warm the catalytic converter to 200°C within 15 seconds of engine start, eliminating cold-start urban emissions.

AdBlue Urea Dosing vs Catalytic Cleaning Efficiency
Optimizes urea injection to maximize NOx reduction without chemical ammonia slip
Urea Dosing
What This Means for the Company:

Injecting 45-60 mg/s of AdBlue delivers maximum 96% NOx conversion. Over-dosing beyond 75 mg/s creates unreacted ammonia smell without improving emissions.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Deploy smart neural dosing controllers that inject the exact chemical stoichiometric amount of AdBlue based on live NOx sensor readings.
