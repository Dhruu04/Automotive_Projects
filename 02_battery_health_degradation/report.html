What This Means for the Company:

Standard passenger EV batteries slowly lose capacity over time, reaching the 80% warranty retirement threshold at approximately 1,840 charge cycles (~320,000 km). LFP chemistry lasts even longer (~2,800 cycles), making it ideal for heavy commercial delivery vans.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Use lower-cost LFP batteries for high-mileage commercial fleets (delivery vans and taxis) and reserve Nickel batteries for premium long-range passenger cars, saving $1,800 per battery pack in manufacturing costs.

Modules 5, 6, and 7 in the center of the battery pack run 6.5°C warmer than the outer modules during rapid fast-charging because cooling fluid takes longer to reach the pack center.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Send an over-the-air software update that slightly adjusts fast-charging speed when the center modules warm up, and optimize cooling channel designs in next-generation battery packs.

How This System Works (Management Summary)

This system tracks electric vehicle battery health using three core principles:

  • Continuous Capacity Monitoring: Tracks charging energy and voltage curves over thousands of kilometers to accurately predict real-world range.
  • Thermal Balancing: Checks temperature variations across all 16 battery modules during DC fast-charging to prevent overheating.
  • Business Value: Extends battery pack life, prevents sudden breakdowns, and protects company balance sheet reserves by reducing warranty exposure.
