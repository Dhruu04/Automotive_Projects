In-Tread Piezo Sensor Vibration (G) vs Slip Angle
Measures micro-acceleration pulses as each tread block enters and exits the road contact patch
Piezo Vibration
What This Means for the Company:

As cornering slip angle rises, tread block deformation vibration jumps from 150 G to over 480 G. This high-frequency acoustic signal identifies whether the road surface is dry asphalt, wet tarmac, or black ice.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Transmit road surface friction classifications over vehicle-to-everything (V2X) cellular networks to warn trailing vehicles of icy bridge decks.

Footprint Sizing
What This Means for the Company:

Under optimal grip, the tire contact patch maintains a stable 175 mm footprint. Footprint contraction below 150 mm signals tire carcass distortion and impending slide.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Display real-time tire contact patch telemetry on supercar track-mode digital displays.
