## 2. Key Operational Findings & Visual Chart Insights
### Carbon Driveshaft Torsional Vibration (G) vs RPM
- **Data Finding:** The one-piece carbon-fiber driveshaft weighs 45% less than steel, eliminating center bearing rumble. At 6,800 RPM, natural torsional vibration rises slightly (0.38 G), well below the 0.42 G NVH limit.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Tune active rear differential rubber mounting bushings to absorb 6,800 RPM micro-harmonics, preserving luxury cabin quietness.

### Dynamic Torsional Stiffness vs Driveline Torque (Nm)
- **Data Finding:** High torsional stiffness (1,800 Nm/rad) ensures instantaneous throttle response and sharp power delivery to rear wheels with zero mechanical flex delay.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Standardize one-piece carbon-fiber driveshafts across all Giorgio platform rear-wheel-drive and all-wheel-drive vehicle variants.

### Torsional Vibration Distribution (G)
- **Data Finding:** Median driveline vibration settles at a low 0.22 G, verifying perfect rotational balance at Balocco testing speeds.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Apply precision laser dynamic balancing on factory assembly lines, eliminating $1.8M in dealership driveline vibration complaints.

### Average Vibration Across Driveshaft RPM Brackets
- **Data Finding:** Vibration averages under 0.28 G across 4,500-6,000 RPM, delivering immediate Italian sports sedan throttle responsiveness.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Highlight carbon-fiber driveshaft engineering as a key luxury sales differentiator against competitors using heavy two-piece steel shafts.
