What This Means for the Company:

Carbon-ceramic matrix (CCM) brake discs withstand 700°C with negligible wear (<2 mg). Above 800°C, atmospheric oxygen begins oxidizing internal carbon fibers into carbon dioxide gas, slowly reducing disc mass.
