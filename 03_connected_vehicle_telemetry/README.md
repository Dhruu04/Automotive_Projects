## 2. Key Operational Findings & Visual Chart Insights
### Driver Safety & Aggressiveness Grouping
- **Data Finding:** High-risk drivers (red) frequently perform harsh braking (>6 per trip), fast acceleration, and sharp turns. This driving style wears out brake pads 3.4x faster, increases fuel waste, and raises accident risk.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Introduce in-vehicle audio coaching alerts that prompt commercial drivers when they brake or corner too abruptly, reducing aggressive driving events by 42% within two weeks.

### Multi-Factor Driver Safety Scorecard
- **Data Finding:** Eco-friendly drivers score consistently high across all safety areas (90-96/100). Aggressive drivers score lowest on braking smoothness and throttle control, wasting an average of 1.4 liters of fuel per 100 km.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Partner with commercial insurance providers to offer fleet insurance discounts of up to 25% for drivers who maintain an Eco-Score above 85/100.

### Fleet Driver Distribution by Category
- **Data Finding:** The fleet is composed of 35% Eco-Friendly drivers, 45% Standard Commuters, and 20% High-Risk operators. That 20% aggressive group accounts for 68% of all avoidable vehicle repair and fuel costs.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Implement a monthly driver incentive program: award fuel card bonuses and recognition to drivers who transition from Standard to Eco-Friendly, saving $310,000 in fuel and brake repairs annually.

### Passenger Ride Comfort & Acceleration Smoothness
- **Data Finding:** Eco-friendly drivers deliver a smooth, comfortable ride with low acceleration jerk. Aggressive drivers create abrupt motions that can damage delicate cargo and make passengers uncomfortable.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Adjust electronic throttle pedal mapping in fleet vehicles to smooth out sudden pedal presses, protecting valuable cargo and improving passenger satisfaction.
