Aerodynamics
What This Means for the Company:

Downforce scales smoothly from 45 kg at 100 km/h to 485 kg at 280 km/h as the rear wing tilts to 14 degrees, keeping high-speed highway stability rock-solid.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Implement automated aerodynamic drag reduction (DRS) on straightaways: flatten the wing to 2 degrees during full throttle above 220 km/h to gain +8 km/h top speed.

Tire Tread Temperature vs Cornering Force
Tracks tire thermal buildup during aggressive track cornering
Thermal Grip
What This Means for the Company:

Tires operate at peak mechanical grip between 90°C and 105°C. Excessive sliding pushes temperatures past 118°C, causing rubber blistering and grip loss.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Display real-time tire thermal gauges on the digital instrument cluster to guide drivers on when to cool tires on cooldown laps.

Wing Angles
What This Means for the Company:

The wing operates mostly between 4 and 14 degrees, pitching up to 18 degrees as an airbrake during heavy emergency braking.

Recommended Management Action & Cost Savings:

Standardize active aerodynamic dual-actuator motors across all GT3 and Turbo models to ensure responsive sub-100ms wing adjustments.
