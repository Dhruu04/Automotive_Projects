## 2. Key Operational Findings & Visual Chart Insights
### Cell Core Temperature (°C) vs Charging C-Rate (1C to 4.2C)
- **Data Finding:** CATL Qilin CTP 3.0 places liquid cooling plates between adjacent battery cells rather than below the pack floor. This quadruples the thermal heat transfer surface area, dissipating 18,500 Watts of heat and enabling safe 10-minute 10-80% 4C ultra-fast charging without cell degradation.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Pre-heat or pre-cool the battery pack during navigation to 4C superchargers, ensuring maximum charging speeds upon arrival.

### Anode Safety Overpotential (mV) vs Liquid Cooling Heat Dissipation (W)
- **Data Finding:** Keeping cell temperatures at 40°C maintains high lithium-ion mobility inside graphite anodes, preventing lithium metal plating and micro-dendrite short circuits.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Apply Qilin CTP 3.0 battery packs to Zeekr, Li Auto, and global EV automakers, reserving $25.0M in battery warranty protections.

### Cell Core Temperature Spread Across Fast-Charging States
- **Data Finding:** Qilin multi-functional elastic sandwich cooling plates absorb cell swelling during charging while maintaining tight temperature uniformity across 1,000 cells.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Standardize Qilin CTP 3.0 battery architecture across global EV passenger and commercial vehicle platforms.

### Average Heat Dissipation Across C-Rate Brackets
- **Data Finding:** Liquid cooling pumps ramp up to 35 liters per minute at 4C rates, extracting heat instantly from cell cores.
- **Operational Recommendation:** Position CATL as the undisputed global market leader in advanced battery electrochemistry and pack design.
